# Bloom Boom 3D — assets

- Flower vending machine and attached booth: user's original `macanic.blend`. Exported via installed Blender to `Assets/Models/Macanic.fbx`; original geometry, named screens, door meshes, buttons, casing and sign preserved. Hidden Boolean operands are not visible assets. Unity axis conversion makes the front face the player.
- Mall panorama: **Royal Esplanade**, Poly Haven, CC0. Source: https://polyhaven.com/a/royal_esplanade . Download: https://dl.polyhaven.org/file/ph-assets/HDRIs/hdr/2k/royal_esplanade_2k.hdr . Used as distant indoor surroundings; the walkable mall, shops, floors and furniture are actual scene geometry.
- Bouquets: original procedural 3D meshes created for this project (stems, leaves, petals, florist paper and ribbons). Catalogue images render the identical meshes stocked in the compartments.
- Music and sound: original synthesized piano arpeggio, confirmation chime, shutter and printer effects generated by `BloomArtBuilder.cs`.
- Update 2.0: nine botanical mesh variants, camera-relative 3D hands, folding wallet and separate debit card are original procedural project assets. Four marble-floor footsteps and a leather rustle are synthesized locally; no external sound licences required.
- Windows icon: `Assets/Art/UI/BloomBoomIcon.png`, created using the built-in image-generation tool (imagegen skill), not the API/CLI fallback. Final prompt: "Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: Windows desktop game application icon for Bloom Boom, a romantic flower vending machine game. A single polished toy-like 3D bouquet of three lush pink and coral roses and small ivory blossoms with deep green leaves, wrapped in cream florist paper, blush pink ribbon bow. Centered bold recognizable silhouette on a deep forest green rounded-square tile, subtle bevel, soft warm studio lighting. Full square icon artwork, ample small safe margin, legible at tiny 32px size, no text, no letters, no watermark, no scene, no additional objects. Square 1024x1024 composition."
- Photo: `Assets/Resources/PhotoboothMemory.png`, generated in the earlier iteration with the built-in image-generation tool. Prompt: a funny but sweet photobooth portrait of an adult man holding an oversized bright mixed bouquet, awkward proud smile, pink-and-cream floral curtain, realistic instant film photo, warm flash, no logos/text/watermark or recognizable real person.
